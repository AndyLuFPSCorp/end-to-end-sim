ReachPour is at the Nova-5's limit: only a 55° tip, lip pointing awayEasy: claw points down; lid edge is close to the robot

DumpingBottom door (design TBD); gravity empties it fullyTilt platform: 45° empties nothing (fries rest against the low wall); needs ~120°, with the lid held open and the cooler clamped
RTDThrough the stainless wall, tip 40 mm above the floorDrilled through the PU wall, sealed with a cable gland + sealant; cable must flex with the tilt
HygieneStainless scoop and coolerSilicone fingers must be food-grade and rated for −18 °C contact; more parts to clean
PayloadScoop + 1 kg fries ≈ 2 kg (Nova-5: 5 kg)Adapter + two hands (weights not yet known) + ~0.2 kg fries
HardwareCustom fabrication: cooler, lid cylinder, bottom doorCatalogue parts (ROCHU + SMC) + tilt platform and drive
